How It Works

The Mission

StagQuest is a 9-day novena that turns your daily discipline into a strike against human trafficking. Every "no" to temptation weakens the $150B exploitation industry.

Daily Prayers (7 per day)

Each day follows the Liturgy of the Hours. Tap each hour to pray, receive a virtue card, and answer a teaching question:

  • Lauds – Morning Prayer (6am)
  • Prime – Early Morning (7am)
  • Terce – Mid-Morning (9am)
  • Sext – Midday (12pm)
  • None – Afternoon (3pm)
  • Vespers – Evening (5pm)
  • Compline – Night (9pm)

EZ Merit Points

Each prayer earns 1 merit point. Correct answers earn 1 more. Complete all 9 days for a 9-point bonus. Points count across all Zoseco games.

Why It Matters

40 million people are trafficked worldwide. 76% are under 18. 42% of family trafficking involves pornography. Your personal discipline is not just about you — it strikes at the demand that fuels this evil.
